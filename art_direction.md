# Art Direction - Robstradomus Idle

- **Style**: Detailed 2D pixel art with a tactical RPG aesthetic, inspired by classic grid-based fantasy games.
- **Wizard (Robstradomus)**: A purple-robed wizard with a pointy hat and a glowing staff, facing forward/three-quarter view.
- **Monsters**: 
    - Slime (Green, gooey)
    - Skeleton (Bony, holding a rusted sword)
    - Dragon (Red, small but fierce for early maps)
- **Environment**: 
    - Grass tiles (Vibrant green with small flowers)
    - Desert tiles (Sandy with cracked earth)
    - Dungeon tiles (Dark stone with moss)
- **UI**: Sleek dark panels with gold trim, clear icons for resources (Gold, Mana Shards, Ancient Rubies).
- **Lighting**: Dappled sunlight on grass, harsh sun in desert, dim torch-lit dungeon.
- **VFX**: Glowing magic missiles, small explosions, and level-up sparkles.
