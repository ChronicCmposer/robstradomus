# Sound Direction - Robstradomus Idle

- **Background Music**: A loopable, 15-second relaxing yet mystical orchestral track with soft harps and flutes.
- **Combat Sound Effects**:
    - `magic-bolt`: A high-pitched magical "zip" or "pew" sound for the wizard's attack.
    - `enemy-hit`: A satisfying "thump" or "splat" for when a monster takes damage.
- **UI Sound Effects**:
    - `upgrade-buy`: A clear "cha-ching" or crystalline chime.
    - `whimsical-celebration`: A playful storybook reward cue with celesta, bouncy pizzicato, tiny magical pops, and fairy-dust sparkle for hero level-ups, achievement milestones, discoveries, and other major rewards.
- **Playback**: Audio starts on first player interaction (e.g., clicking "Start Farming"). Celebration volume is independently adjustable in Settings.
